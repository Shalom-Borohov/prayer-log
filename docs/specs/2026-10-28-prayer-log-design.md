# תפילות — Prayer & Torah Log PWA Design

Personal iPhone PWA for logging each day's prayers (במניין / ביחידות / ק״ש בלבד) and Torah learning, with totals, streaks and per-prayer stats. Sibling of אימונים (`~/Personal/workout-log`).

## Decisions (from grilling, 2026-10-28)

1. **Prayers:** שחרית, מנחה, ערבית every day. A מוסף row appears only on days it applies (שבת, ראש חודש, יום טוב, חול המועד, ראש השנה, יום כיפור), by the Israel holiday schedule (see 38). No סליחות or נעילה.
2. **Per prayer:** במניין / ביחידות / not marked (see 16 for ק״ש בלבד). Tapping the selected state again clears it.
3. **Counters:** weekly and monthly totals, streaks, and per-prayer stats.
4. **Torah:** a "למדתי תורה" checkbox plus an optional one-line "מה למדתי" note.
5. **Streaks:** a separate streak for each daily prayer (consecutive days במניין), shown next to that prayer on היום, plus a Torah streak (consecutive days with למדתי) on the Torah row. ביחידות or a blank breaks a prayer streak. Today never breaks a streak before the day ends. מוסף has no streak; its counts appear in stats. Stats also show each streak's best ever.
6. **Day boundary:** calendar day, rolling over at 03:00 (see 38). Logging ערבית at 00:30 lands on the previous day. Friday-night ערבית counts as Friday.
7. **Layout:** three tabs, היום · לוח · סטטיסטיקה. היום has ‹ › to step through past days (never into the future), and any day opens from לוח.
8. **Calendar:** Gregorian month grid with small Hebrew dates and holiday / ראש חודש names. "This month" stats use the Gregorian month.
9. **Backfill banner:** on היום, a row such as "שבת לא מולאה" for each day in the last 7 that has nothing marked at all; tapping it opens that day. Partly filled days count as missed and don't show the banner (except the eve row, see 13). See also 31 and 37.
10. **Name:** תפילות. Repo and folder: `prayer-log`.
11. **Voice (impeccable init):** quiet and factual. Numbers and plain labels, no praise and no guilt.
12. **Logging moment (impeccable init):** usually right after each prayer, one-handed; sometimes catching up at night.
13. **Eve ערבית reminder (added 2026-10-28, extends 9):** the banner also lists "ערבית של <day> לא סומנה" for a partly filled day (see 37) in the last 7 whose next day is שבת or an Israeli יום טוב and whose ערבית is blank, since that ערבית can only be logged after שבת / the חג. Rows are ordered oldest first. Decision 6 is unchanged: that ערבית still belongs to the eve.
14. **Visual world (impeccable, 2026-10-28):** The Beit Midrash Shelf. Each prayer owns a flat leather hue on every tab; gold leaf is the במניין state, and a ticked Torah spine is gold too (`DESIGN.md`, The Gold Means Minyan Rule); a 7-day "shelf" of spines per row (gold-stamped = במניין, bare leather = ביחידות, empty slot = unmarked; see 17 for ק״ש בלבד). Dark leather header band with gold title. Contract: `.impeccable/surfaces/src-app-tsx.md`.
15. **Calendar holiday names (added 2026-10-28, clarifies 8):** religious holidays, ראש חודש, fasts and minor holidays, plus the national days יום השואה, יום הזכרון, יום העצמאות, יום ירושלים and the חרבות ברזל memorial day. Civil observances (יום רבין, יום בן־גוריון, school days, etc.) are left out.
16. **ק״ש בלבד (added 2026-10-29, extends 2):** שחרית and ערבית get a third state, ק״ש בלבד, for a day when only קריאת שמע was said instead of the prayer. It is a third segment (במניין | ביחידות | ק״ש בלבד); tapping it again clears it. מנחה and מוסף have no קריאת שמע and keep two states. Like ביחידות, it breaks the במניין streak and never counts as במניין.
17. **Short spine (added 2026-10-29, extends 14):** a ק״ש בלבד mark is a half-height bare leather spine on every shelf (היום, לוח cells, סטטיסטיקה).
18. **תפילין and טלית (added 2026-10-29):** one check each per day, inside the שחרית row. תפילין is hidden on שבת, יום טוב (ראש השנה and יום כיפור included) and חול המועד (הושענא רבה included), following the Israeli custom; it shows on ט׳ באב, when they are worn at מנחה. טלית shows every day.
19. **Counts (added 2026-10-29, extends 3):** השבוע on היום lists ק״ש בלבד, תפילין and טלית alongside the existing rows; סטטיסטיקה adds a ק״ש column to the per-prayer table and a תפילין וטלית group. The תפילין denominator counts only days they are worn. No streaks or shelves for תפילין and טלית.
20. **One leather block (added 2026-10-29, clarifies 14):** the header band is one solid block from the status bar through the date line. It never shows the page ground during iOS page transitions, never fades while scrolling, and stays leather when the page is pulled down. On iOS 26 the installed app keeps the status bar leather too (clarified 2026-10-30): Safari ignores `theme-color` there and colours it from the page background or a fixed bar at the top edge, so both are leather. The fixed bar must take pointer events (clarified 2026-10-31): Safari skips a `pointer-events: none` bar and draws a soft haze over the top of the header instead.
21. **בית כנסת (added 2026-10-29, extends 2):**
    - A prayer marked במניין can carry the place it was said in. Marking במניין stays one tap.
    - The prayer's default place is filled in automatically: the place from the latest day on which that prayer has one. Each prayer keeps its own default, so מנחה's place doesn't carry over to ערבית.
    - If the prayer has no default yet, an action sheet opens to pick one.
    - The place shows quietly beside the prayer name. Tapping it opens the action sheet:
      - places used before, most recent first;
      - מקום חדש…;
      - ללא מקום;
      - ביטול.
    - Switching the prayer to ביחידות or ק״ש בלבד, or clearing it, removes the place.
    - The list of places is derived from the logged days: no separate list, no renaming, no per-place stats.
22. **Version line (added 2026-10-29, changed 2026-10-30):** the bottom of הגדרות shows "גרסה <version> · עודכנה <build date and time>", so it's easy to tell whether the phone has the latest deploy.
    - The version is the semver in `package.json`, starting at 1.0.0. Every PR that ships raises it: the minor number for features, the patch number for fixes.
    - The build time is stamped in at build time.
23. **Report a bug or idea (added 2026-10-30):** הגדרות has a "דיווח על באג / הצעה" row.
    - It opens a sheet with a באג / הצעה switch and a description field.
    - "פתיחה ב-GitHub" opens a prefilled new issue in the public prayer-log repo, labelled `bug` or `feature`. The title is the first line of the text, cut at 80 characters with "…".
    - The issue carries the browser's user agent, the version and the build time. No prayer data is ever sent.
24. **Updates (added 2026-10-31):**
    - The app checks for a new version when it opens, when it comes back from the background, and every hour while open. It skips the check while offline.
    - A downloaded version waits. A "גרסה חדשה זמינה · עדכון" bar sits above the tab bar on every tab, and "עדכון" reloads into the new version.
    - × hides the bar until the app comes back from the background. An ignored update applies the next time the app is fully closed and reopened.
25. **מה חדש (added 2026-10-31, extends 22):**
    - After an update, a "מה חדש" sheet opens once with every release since the last one seen, newest first, each with "חדש" and "תיקונים". With nothing seen before, it shows only the current release.
    - Tapping the version line in הגדרות opens the sheet with every release.
    - The last seen version is kept on the phone and is not in the backup.
26. **כל הזמנים and the trend graph (added 2026-11-02, extends 3 and 14):**
    - סטטיסטיקה switches between השבוע, החודש and כל הזמנים. כל הזמנים runs from the first day with any record to today (assumption 3).
    - Above the per-prayer table, a graph shows one column per day (השבוע), per Sunday-start week clipped to the month (החודש), or for כל הזמנים per week while the record spans at most 12 weeks and per month after that (until decision 46 applies).
    - Every column with prayers due is full height, so a period still in progress or starting mid-way doesn't look like a drop. It shows shares of the prayers due, counted like the tables (מוסף included, nothing before the first record or after today): gold for במניין, plain leather (not a prayer hue) for ביחידות and ק״ש בלבד together, and the dashed rest for unmarked. Unlike the tables (assumption 4), the graph counts today's prayers only once they are marked, so prayers still ahead today never show as a gap. Days of the week still ahead keep an empty slot. The counts stay in the tables and in the graph's spoken label, which names a week by its first and last day.
    - The period shelves show on השבוע and החודש only.
27. **Bottom navigation bar (added 2026-11-02, clarifies 7):** the ‹ › that step a day on היום (and on a day opened from לוח) and a month on לוח sit in a bar just above the tab bar, in reach of the thumb, as "הקודם" and "הבא". The header keeps only the title, date line, back button and gear.
28. **Swipe between days and months (added 2026-11-03, extends 7 and 27):** a horizontal swipe anywhere in the content does what הקודם and הבא do, on a day (היום and a day opened from לוח) and on a month in לוח. Dragging left goes to הקודם and dragging right to הבא, matching the bar's right-to-left order.
    - The content under the date line follows the finger; the header, the bottom bar and the tab bar stay still (decision 20). Past a third of the screen width, or on a flick in the same direction, the content slides off and the new day or month slides in from the other side. A shorter drag springs back.
    - Toward the future on today or the current month, the content moves with heavy resistance and springs back (decision 7).
    - The iOS edge swipe-back on a day opened from לוח still goes back to לוח. With reduce motion on, the content changes without sliding.
    - A drag that starts in the מה למדתי field stays with the field, so moving the caret or selecting text never changes the day.
29. **Gear on every page (added 2026-11-03, clarifies 27 and assumption 2):** every page except הגדרות has the gear at the end of its toolbar. הגדרות opens inside the current tab, and its back button, labelled with the tab's name, returns to the page it was opened from (see 48).
30. **תפילין and טלית in לוח (added 2026-11-06, extends 19):** each לוח cell has a line under its spines with a small square for תפילין and a small bar for טלית, in the שחרית hue, in the same order as their checks. A mark shows only when the item is marked on a day it applies (decision 18). Otherwise its place stays blank, so marks never move and rows stay the same height. These are not shelf slots: they have no empty state, no streak and no gold.
31. **Backup reminder (added 2026-11-06, extends 9 and 25):** the data lives only on the phone, so היום says when a backup is overdue. It stays inside the app (assumption 6).
    - On today only, the banner ends with "הגיבוי האחרון לפני 8 ימים", or "עדיין לא נעשה גיבוי" if there has never been one.
    - A backup is due 7 logical days after the last one (decision 6), the same reach as the backfill rows. With none yet, the days count from the first record (assumption 3). An empty app shows no row, and nothing checks whether the data changed since the last backup.
    - Tapping the row opens the share sheet, with no stop in הגדרות. There is no ×: the row blocks nothing and one tap clears it.
    - An export from the row or from הגדרות counts as a backup once the share completes. iOS reports only that, not where the file went, so sharing anywhere counts. A cancel changes nothing. A failure shows "הפעולה נכשלה", never the browser's own message (decision 11), and keeps the row. A second tap while the share is open does nothing, since a second share would fail over a working one.
    - Where the browser can't share a file, the export downloads it and counts as a backup at once, since the browser reports nothing further. This happens only outside the installed iPhone app, and a reminder that can't be cleared would be worse.
    - A successful import counts as a backup made at the file's export time, so restoring on a new phone doesn't claim there was never a backup. An older file moves the time back, because the phone now holds that older data, and one tap clears the row. A file dated in the future counts as made now, so it can't silence the reminder.
    - הגדרות shows "גיבוי אחרון: <date and time>" or "עדיין לא נעשה גיבוי" in the גיבוי section.
    - The last backup time is kept on the phone and is not in the backup.
32. **Recovery screen (added 2026-11-07):** if a page can't draw, for example because a stored record is malformed, the app shows a plain "משהו השתבש" screen instead of a blank page, with "ייצוא גיבוי" and "טעינה מחדש". The data lives only on the phone, so a bad record must never cut off the way out. Bad records aren't skipped on read, and the app never deletes or rewrites them on its own, because only the owner can decide what a damaged record was. The screen has no bug report link, so nothing from a record can reach a public repo. "טעינה מחדש" opens the app at its start, not on the page that failed: a Home Screen app has no address bar, so reloading that page would only bring the screen back.
33. **A backup with a bad record (added 2026-11-07, extends 31):** an export checks each record with the import's own rules. The file is still produced, so no data becomes unreachable, and a message says how many records are bad and that importing the file will fail, because finding that out on the day of a restore is too late. The message shows even if the share is cancelled, because the damage is in the data and not in the file. A completed share still counts as a backup under decision 31, from הגדרות, the reminder row or the recovery screen.
34. **A failed save (added 2026-11-07):** when a write fails, the mark isn't shown as done and "לא נשמר. אפשר לנסות שוב." appears as a toast, so nothing ever looks saved when it isn't. Marks already show what's stored. A במניין mark that didn't save doesn't go on to ask for a place.
35. **מה למדתי saves as you type (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 4):** every change to the note is written at once, with no return or blur needed, because the app is used for a few seconds at a time and swiping to another day or closing the app must not lose what was typed. The stored note is trimmed, but the field keeps the typed text until it loses focus, so a space between words isn't taken away mid-sentence. While writes keep failing, decision 34's message shows at most once per toast length, not once per letter, and the field goes back to what's stored when it loses focus. Until then it keeps the unsaved text, because losing typed text mid-sentence is worse.
36. **היום on a new day (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 7):** when the app comes back from the background on a later logical day (decision 6), a היום tab left on a past day returns to today, because marking a prayer is one tap and that tap must not land on an old day. It stays on the past day if the logical day hasn't changed, and it doesn't move while the app stays open across 03:00. If the app comes back on another tab, it stays there and the היום tab opens on today (see 48 for הגדרות). A day opened from לוח stays where it is.
37. **A note isn't a mark (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 9 and 13):** the banner goes by marks: a prayer, למדתי, תפילין or טלית. A day with only a מה למדתי note has nothing marked, so it gets decision 9's row, matching לוח, streaks and totals, which already treat it as unmarked. A note-only eve gets that one row, not 13's as well. The note is still stored and backed up, and it still counts as a record for assumption 3, so a note-only first day starts tracking and gets a row.
38. **Abroad (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 1 and 6):** the app has no location and no setting, so the Israel schedule applies everywhere: a day that is יום טוב only abroad shows no מוסף. The 03:00 rollover follows the phone's clock, so it moves with the phone's time zone. Flying west can put a logged day in the future for a few hours: it can't be opened until the logical day catches up (decision 7), and its record stays as it was.
39. **A report is kept, and a long one is cut (added 2026-11-07, extends 23):**
    - "פתיחה ב-GitHub" leaves the sheet open with the text, because the app can't tell whether GitHub opened or accepted the report. With `noopener`, `window.open` returns null even on success, and GitHub's own errors are out of reach. The text stays until the owner closes the sheet. It lives only in the open sheet, so it doesn't survive iOS reloading the app while GitHub is open.
    - After the first tap, ביטול becomes סגירה and a note says "אם GitHub לא נפתח, אפשר ללחוץ שוב או להעתיק את הטקסט." Tapping again opens GitHub again. Both stay if the text changes after the tap, because the app can't tell what reached GitHub anyway.
    - The link stays at most 4,300 bytes. Measured signed out on 2026-11-07: real Hebrew keeps the way back to the report after login up to about 4,400 bytes, about 7,300 returns a 500 and about 8,500 a 414. Spaces cost the most when GitHub re-encodes the link for that redirect, so only text of single letters separated by spaces loses the way back below the cap, and the sheet still holds it. Signed in and on the phone are not measured.
    - Typing isn't limited. Over the limit, a note says "הדיווח ארוך מדי לקישור. ב-GitHub תופיע רק ההתחלה, ואת השאר אפשר להעתיק מכאן ולהדביק שם." before any tap, and goes away once the text fits.
    - A cut report keeps as many whole characters as fit and ends "… (הדיווח נחתך כאן)". The device details are always kept, and the title still comes from the full text (decision 23). The title's 80 characters are counted as code points, because a single whole character can be any length and must not use up the link.
40. **The last seen version has its own key (added 2026-11-07, extends 25):** the last seen version is stored under `prayer-log:seen-version`, because another app on the same origin also used `seen-version` and could keep "מה חדש" quiet here. The old key is still read while the new one is missing, so a phone that skipped a release still gets every release since the last one seen. A value in the old key above the running version is ignored, because it can't have come from this app, and the sheet then shows the current release as on a first install. The new key is only ever written by this app, so it is trusted as is. A lower or equal value from the other app can't be told apart from this app's own, so on a shared origin the first sheet may list extra releases or stay closed for one release, which is harmless and ends once the new key is written. The old key is never written or removed, because the other app may still use it.
41. **Errors use the app's own words (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 11, generalises the failure rule in 31):** the app never shows the browser's own error text. An invalid backup file says "קובץ הגיבוי לא תקין", and any other failure says "הפעולה נכשלה" unless a decision gives it its own message, because browser messages are in English and mean nothing to the owner.
42. **Updating from the recovery screen (added 2026-11-07, extends 24 and 32):** while a new version is waiting, the recovery screen says "גרסה חדשה זמינה" and "טעינה מחדש" becomes "עדכון", which opens the new version at the app's start. Reloading would only bring back the same broken build, and a broken release must not need a full close of the app to get off it. The screen checks for updates on decision 24's schedule. There is no ×, since nothing is in the way, and the update isn't applied without a tap, so the screen can't disappear in the middle of an export.
43. **Importing from the recovery screen (added 2026-11-07, extends 31 and 32):** the recovery screen has "ייבוא גיבוי", because a crash on היום makes הגדרות unreachable and a bad record could otherwise stay until an app update. The file gets the same checks as in הגדרות (decision 33 still applies: a file with a bad record is rejected as a whole). Before anything changes, the screen says "הייבוא יחליף את כל הנתונים. כדאי לייצא גיבוי קודם." with "ייבוא" and "ביטול", because the days logged since that backup are lost. The confirmation is on the screen itself, not an alert, so the screen stays free of Ionic. Importing is the owner's choice, so it doesn't conflict with decision 32's rule that the app never rewrites records on its own. A successful import counts as a backup under decision 31 and opens the app at its start, with no message, since the working app shows it worked. If the write fails, decision 34's message shows and the stored data stays as it was. If only the backup time can't be stored, the import still counts as done, because the data was replaced and saying otherwise would invite a second import. Once "ייבוא" is tapped, "ייבוא" and "ביטול" stop responding until the write ends, so it can't run twice or go on after a "ביטול". The other buttons stay live, because the write is one transaction and a reload leaves either the old data or the new. "ביטול" also clears the failure message, because there is nothing left to try again. On this screen failures show as a line of text above the import buttons, because there is no toast here, and a file failure stays until the next file is chosen.
44. **Screen reader names (added 2026-11-07, clarifies assumption 3 and decision 26):** a shelf, a graph, a לוח cell and an icon each get a name that's true and not empty.
    - A shelf's spoken count leaves out days before tracking started, so it reads "1 מתוך 2", because those days are not missed (assumption 3). An unmarked today still counts (assumption 4). With one tracked day it reads "1 מתוך יום אחד", since "מתוך 1 הימים האחרונים" isn't Hebrew.
    - A shelf with no tracked day, and a graph with no column to speak (a Sunday morning on השבוע, or the 1st of the month on החודש), read "עוד אין נתונים", because an image with no name says nothing and "0 מתוך 0" means nothing.
    - A לוח cell's name starts with what the cell shows: "1 כ״א בחשון, יום ראשון: לא סומן". Voice Control can then find it by its visible text, and VoiceOver hears the date once. The Gregorian month comes from the page title. A cell before tracking started or in the future still reads "לא סומן": it says the day has no mark, not that it was missed (assumption 3).
    - An icon beside a label or inside a labelled button is decoration and is hidden from screen readers.
45. **How targets reach 44pt (added 2026-11-07, clarifies assumption 7):** a tap counts across the full 44pt even where a control looks smaller, because a missed tap costs more than anything else in a few-second, one-handed use.
    - Controls keep their look wherever the tap area can grow invisibly: the במניין / ביחידות segments (40 inside a 44 track), toolbar buttons, הקודם and הבא, and the update bar's × and עדכון.
    - Ionic's segment switch clips its contents and ignores taps on its track, so the tap area can't grow invisibly. The period switch on סטטיסטיקה and the באג / הצעה switch are visibly 44 tall, matching the attendance control.
    - לוח cells stay about 43 wide on the narrowest phones, because seven must fit across. They are far taller than 44.
46. **Long histories in the trend graph (added 2026-11-07, extends 26):** כל הזמנים goes per Gregorian quarter once months would take more than 36 columns, and per Gregorian year once quarters would, because the fixed gaps and dashed borders leave no room for gold and leather in narrower columns. The first column still starts at the first record. Under quarters, the axis shows the year on the column of each January–March, and the spoken label names a quarter by its calendar months, such as ינואר–מרץ 2027. Under years, the axis labels every Nth column so that at most seven labels show, as under months, so the current year may have no label, and the spoken label is the year alone. The group heading names the unit: לפי רבעון, לפי שנה. Years have no coarser unit, which is safe in practice: the fill lasts until about 52 yearly columns on a 375px screen.
47. **לוח on a new month (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 8 and 27):** a לוח showing the current month moves to the new month once the logical day (decision 6) crosses into it, with today marked, without sliding. A past month you went back to stays where it is, because looking at an old month does no harm and nothing should move away from what you chose to see. Stepping onto the current month makes לוח follow the date again. A לוח under a day opened from it moves too, so back shows the current month. If the phone's clock moves back, the month on screen stays until the next step.
48. **הגדרות on a new day (added 2026-11-07, clarifies 29 and 36):** הגדרות opened from a past day on היום stays open when the app comes back on a later logical day, because it's the page in use. Its back button then ends on today and not on the past day, because the next tap must not land on an old day (decision 36). On the same logical day, or when opened from a day opened from לוח, it returns to the page it was opened from (decision 29). If the app comes back on another tab, the היום tab reopens on הגדרות, as an iOS tab reopens on the page you left, and its back button still ends on today.

## Assumptions (accepted by the user)

1. Stack: same as אימונים — React 19 + TypeScript + Vite, Ionic React in iOS mode, Dexie with persistent storage, `vite-plugin-pwa`, Vitest + Testing Library + fake-indexeddb, oxlint. Hebrew calendar via `@hebcal/core` (offline, no location).
2. Data: one `DayRecord` per logical date `{ date: 'YYYY-MM-DD', prayers: Partial<Record<Prayer, 'minyan' | 'alone' | 'shema'>>, torah: boolean, torahNote?: string, tefillin?: boolean, tallit?: boolean, places?: Partial<Record<Prayer, string>> }`; `tefillin` and `tallit` are stored only when true, and `places` holds a name only for a prayer marked במניין.
